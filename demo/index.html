One-Step Token-to-Waveform Generation with MeanFlow in Latent Space

Audio Demo Page — Interspeech 2026 Submission

Abstract: We propose a novel Token2Wav architecture that applies MeanFlow in a highly compressed latent space, enabling true one-step generation with a single network evaluation. Operating in the latent domain yields up to a 17× RTF improvement over multi-step baselines with negligible quality degradation. We further introduce refinement strategies—decoder-only and end-to-end joint fine-tuning—to mitigate latent mismatch.
